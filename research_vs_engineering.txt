1. KV CACHE COMPRESSION/QUANTIZATION FOR CROSS-VENDOR TRANSFER
- Can you compress KV cache to INT4/FP8 for transfer, then decompress on MI300X?
- Maybe INT4 works fine for 7B but degrades 70B. Maybe FP8 is fine but INT4 breaks long-context generation. (uncertainty)
- Need to evaluate on perplexity, MMLU, HumanEval, long-context benchmarks across multiple model sizes
