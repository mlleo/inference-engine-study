    CRITICAL CAVEATS
    ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
    
    1. LOAD MISMATCH: H100 is handling 60x fewer total requests than MI300x. All throughput comparisons (intoken throughput, outtoken throughput, requests/pod) are confounded. The latency and TPOT comparisons are more trustworthy because they're per-request/per-token metrics that are less sensitive to load.
    
    2. DECODE OUTPUT LENGTH MISMATCH: H100 decode generates 947 tokens/req, MI300x generates 730. The 30% difference makes e2e latency comparison misleading. TPOT is the fair metric here, and MI300x wins by 13%.
    
    3. MISSING TTFT FOR PREFILL: Both prefill rows show "-" for TTFT. TTFT is the most important prefill user-facing metric. You should fix the tracking issue — without it, you're using e2e as a proxy, which includes queue time.
    
    4. H100 UNDERUTILIZATION: H100 prefill runs 0.0147 req/sec/pod and H100 decode runs 0.00811 req/sec/pod. These are very low. The H100 latency numbers are from a lightly loaded system. Under heavier load, H100 latency would increase (more queueing, larger batches). The 2.3x prefill advantage and 2.46x TTFT advantage may shrink under equal load.
    
    5. KV CACHE TRANSFER: In the heterogeneous setup, you'll need to transfer KV caches from H100 prefill pods to MI300x decode pods across different GPU architectures. This adds network overhead not captured in either baseline. Budget for this.
